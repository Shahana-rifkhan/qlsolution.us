Academic Support FAQs

Know what to expect before you begin.

Clear answers about the type of help available, how sessions work, and what to share with us.

What should I send for an initial review?

Send the brief or task instructions, marking criteria, academic level, deadline, referencing style, and any work already completed. Also tell us where you feel stuck.

Can you support a dissertation or research project over time?

Yes. Support can be organised around milestones such as the proposal, literature review, methodology, analysis, discussion, presentation, and final quality review.

Do you provide editing and referencing checks?

Yes. We can review grammar, clarity, structure, tone, citation consistency, and document formatting while preserving your meaning and authorship.

Can you help with data or coding?

Yes, subject to the tools and methods involved. Guidance may include analysis planning, code review, debugging, visualisation, interpretation, and explaining technical results.

Will my request remain confidential?

Yes. Personal details, institutional information, and the content shared for support are treated as confidential within the engagement.

Do you complete assessed work for students?

No. Our service is guidance-focused: tutoring, planning, feedback, editing, technical explanation, and coaching. You remain the author and are responsible for following your institution's academic-integrity policies.
